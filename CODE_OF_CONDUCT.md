# Code of Conduct

TagVerity is an open-source project and should be a useful, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy: never post NFC identifiers, credentials, private card data, signing keys, or other sensitive material in issues or pull requests.
- Keep security reports out of public issues when they could expose users; follow `SECURITY.md`.
- Keep contributions inside TagVerity's read-only safety boundary unless maintainers explicitly change that project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, spam, or repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported through the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
